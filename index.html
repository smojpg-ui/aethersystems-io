We research
the humanity in technology.

Aether Systems works where behavioral health, human–machine teaming, and human–AI alignment meet. Aether builds behavioral-health support for crews in isolated, high-stakes environments: spacecraft, analog habitats, and offshore platforms.

§ 02 — The research

Four lines of research. One architecture.

01Conscience Architecture

Models one person against their own history rather than a population norm — locally, on their hardware, under their keys. The framework the other three run on.

02Relational Twin

Mission Chemistry. Carries the framework into long-duration spaceflight as a software payload — no added mass, on-device inference, no ground tether. U.S. provisional patent application filed · Invited speaker, ESA Space for Inspiration 2026.

03Relational Signal Science

A different unit of study: not the person, not the system, but the coupling between them — treated as measurable. Draws on enactivism, second-order cybernetics, and dyadic developmental psychology.

04Project Sanctuary

The same instrument, brought to Earth as the Persistent Operational Twin System. The isolated worksite redesigned around the crew, not the asset: operator-owned behavioral support, biophilic habitat, and on-site clean energy in one system.

§ 03 — The work now

Aether is not asking to use a crew. It is offering to de-risk one.

You bring the crew, the confinement, and the study funding. Aether brings the instrument, protocol, deployment, and analysis — and your crew keeps custody of their own baselines.
